# Workplace Operations Policy (Prototype)

Current time: November 8, 2026, 9:00 AM Pacific.

## General operating rules

1. Search organizational systems for recorded facts. Ask the requester for
   preferences, intent, private context, and authorization that cannot be established
   from those systems.
2. Do not treat the user's initial request as authorization for a consequential
   external action when this policy requires approval of a concrete proposal.
3. When records conflict, use an item marked `current`. If no item is marked current,
   prefer the most recently updated authoritative source and explain the conflict.
4. Complete every requested downstream update. Before reporting completion, verify
   the resulting state in each affected application.

## Meetings and project coordination

1. Before scheduling a meeting, establish its purpose, required attendees, duration,
   and the requester's scheduling preference.
2. The requester must approve the exact meeting proposal before invitations are
   created. Approval applies only to the proposal ID reviewed by the requester.
3. A project with a confirmed external pilot requires internal Legal attendance at
   its kickoff.
4. External pilot participants must not be invited until Legal clearance is recorded.
5. The internal project channel must receive the scheduled time and preparation owner.

## External customer communication

1. Use the verified customer contact stored in CRM. Similar addresses found in email
   or chat are not authoritative.
2. Incident claims must be grounded in the support system's current incident record.
3. Any service-credit amount must be within the approving employee's documented or
   explicitly stated authority. Amounts above that authority require Finance approval.
4. A customer-facing draft must be approved by the account owner before it is sent.
   Approval applies only to the exact draft ID reviewed by the account owner.
5. Customer communication must not promise unapproved compensation or unconfirmed
   remediation dates.
